# EPSILON 30-Day Verifiable Impact Sprint

Start: August 30, 2026
Primary market: United States
Public product: https://epsilonfield.space
Public challenge: https://github.com/DresdenGman/EPSILON-trading-simulator/discussions/8

## Campaign thesis

The campaign is not **“support my product.”** It is:

> **Try to falsify this experiment. Find the first assumption EPSILON still makes too easy to hide.**

This creates a coherent chain from applied mathematics to public contribution:

```text
Sensitivity analysis → runnable instrument → external challenge → public disposition → changed experiment or product
```

The goal is not to maximize attention. The goal is to make other people's independent reasoning enter a permanent, inspectable record.

## Primary outcomes

### 1. Verified External Research Workflows (VERW)

A unique non-author, non-bot person counts once when they:

1. complete a baseline test;
2. inspect at least one perturbation;
3. record a conclusion;
4. identify an assumption, limitation, or failure condition; and
5. leave a durable evidence record.

Accepted records include a public GitHub issue or discussion, independent repository or commit, published evidence artifact, or organizer-confirmed workshop record.

**30-day target:** 10 VERW, including at least 6 voluntarily identified as U.S.-affiliated and at least 3 distinct groups or institutions.

### 2. Independent Reproduction Attempts (IRA)

A unique external person counts once when they test a fixed public release or commit in their own environment and publish `match`, `mismatch`, `partial`, or `unable to complete` with supporting evidence.

**30-day target:** 3 independent attempts. Matching is not required; auditability is.

### 3. Substantive Independent Challenges Resolved (SICR)

A challenge counts when it comes from an independent human, identifies a concrete methodological or product weakness, remains durably linkable, and receives a public disposition.

Allowed dispositions: `ACCEPTED`, `EXPERIMENT QUEUED`, `NOT ACCEPTED` with reasoning, or `DUPLICATE` with a link.

**30-day target:** 5 resolved challenges, with at least 2 producing an accepted weakness, queued experiment, correction, or product change.

## Diagnostic metrics

- **Qualified outreach conversion:** VERW generated by personalized invitations divided by invitations delivered.
- **Evidence completion rate:** distinct sessions completing an evidence field divided by distinct sessions opening the laboratory.
- **Export rate:** distinct sessions exporting an artifact divided by distinct sessions completing one.
- **Challenge continuation rate:** distinct sessions opening the challenge channel divided by distinct sessions completing an evidence field.
- **Channel evidence yield:** `(VERW + IRA)` attributable to a channel divided by its unique referred sessions.

## Guardrails

- Every primary KPI unit has a public URL, artifact, commit, DOI, or organizer confirmation.
- No purchased traffic, reviews, Stars, votes, comments, or endorsements.
- No coordinated launch voting.
- No profitability, alpha, prediction, or investment-advice claims.
- Synthetic evidence remains labeled synthetic.
- Negative and inconclusive findings remain visible.
- No reviewer is named or quoted without permission.
- U.S. affiliation is counted only when voluntarily disclosed; it is never inferred from an IP address.

## First 72 hours

### Hours 0–12 — infrastructure

- Publish the canonical `epsilonfield.space` domain everywhere.
- Enable anonymous first-party events for laboratory open, evidence completion, export, and challenge continuation.
- Publish `/impact` with an explicit hierarchy separating reach, use, challenge, and change.
- Add `CITATION.cff`, Zenodo metadata, contribution guidance, structured reproduction/challenge forms, and automatic daily metric snapshots.
- Submit the public repository to Software Heritage Save Code Now.

### Hours 12–30 — five-person pilot recruitment

Recruit 5–8 people individually:

- 2 with quant or backtesting experience;
- 1 with statistics or research-methods experience;
- 1 research-software or reproducibility reviewer;
- 1 student or educator matching the intended educational use.

Exact assignment:

> Complete one EPSILON evidence field. Export the artifact. Tell me the first conclusion, assumption, or interface behavior you do not trust. I am not asking for endorsement.

Ask at least two people to attempt a clean-environment reproduction using the fixed release.

### Hours 30–48 — evidence funnel check

Launch gate:

- at least 5 outsiders tried the product or provided concrete feedback;
- at least 2 completed a full evidence workflow;
- at least 1 methodological challenge is public;
- the canonical domain, mobile path, export, and challenge links work;
- no unqualified historical-validity or profitability claim remains.

### Hours 48–72 — first broad technical launch

If the gate passes, publish Show HN:

**Title:** `Show HN: EPSILON – test whether a backtest survives small assumption changes`

**Opening question:** `Which assumption here is still too easy for the interface to hide?`

Do not mention college applications, ask for votes, or coordinate comments. Remain available to answer every substantive criticism.

## Days 4–30

### Days 4–7 — first launch and one community post

- Show HN after the pilot gate.
- Choose exactly one methods-oriented Reddit or technical community post after confirming current rules.
- Record referred sessions, completed artifacts, challenges, and reproducibility reports by channel.

### Days 8–14 — two institutional pilots

- Contact ReproducibiliTea chapters, SIAM student chapters, statistics/data-science clubs, and quantitative-finance clubs.
- Offer a free 20-minute exercise, not a product pitch.
- Preserve the public event link or organizer confirmation, attendance, completed workflows, and challenges.
- Freeze one historical-validation protocol before executing it; publish unfavorable results unchanged.

### Days 15–21 — reproducibility sprint

Open bounded contribution tasks:

- reproduce on Windows;
- reproduce on Linux;
- verify deterministic output and evidence hashes;
- challenge the sign-based rejection rule;
- inspect transaction-cost accounting;
- inspect temporal leakage;
- propose one missing perturbation;
- review accessibility and interpretation.

Publish a technical walkthrough titled around reproduction or falsification, not trading returns.

### Days 22–30 — evidence package and secondary launch

- Resolve every substantive challenge publicly.
- Publish a postmortem showing which criticism changed the work.
- Create a meaningful GitHub release only if the evidence workflow or protocol materially changed.
- Connect the release to Zenodo and preserve its DOI.
- Save the final Software Heritage SWHID.
- Consider Product Hunt only as a discovery amplifier after external evidence exists.

## Outreach asks

### University or student chapter

> I built an open-source sensitivity-analysis instrument for a narrow question: when a backtest conclusion looks clean, which nearby assumption makes it stop holding? I am not asking for endorsement. Would your group spend 20 minutes trying to falsify one fixed experiment and leave one public methodological objection or reproduction result?

### Statistics or research-methods educator

> I would value criticism of the pre-specified decision rule, perturbation sizes, replication design, and whether the evidence supports the stated claim. A negative assessment is more useful than praise. Would you or one student test the public workflow and identify the first unjustified inference?

### Research-software reviewer

> Please clone the fixed release in a clean environment and report the first undocumented dependency, nondeterminism, or provenance gap. Match, mismatch, and unable-to-complete are all accepted results.

### Quantitative-finance club

> Bring one conclusion you would normally report from a backtest. Use EPSILON to test what happens when cost, timing, window, or universe assumptions move. The only requested output is the first claim you no longer trust.

## Application evidence package

Maintain one source-of-truth index with:

```text
Claim → exact number → definition → external source URL → archived source or DOI
```

Strongest evidence:

- external reproduction reports and independent forks;
- public objection → disposition → issue/experiment/PR chains;
- organizer-confirmed teaching or club sessions;
- OSF preregistration DOI, Zenodo release DOI, and Software Heritage SWHID, each labeled accurately;
- outside technical writeups or presentations using the method.

Supporting context only:

- Stars, forks, HN points, Product Hunt votes, video views, impressions, and raw traffic.

## Strategy falsifiers

Stop scaling distribution and fix the product or protocol when any occurs:

- 30 qualified invitations produce fewer than 3 completed workflows;
- 10 laboratory starts produce fewer than 3 evidence artifacts;
- a broad channel generates more than 500 visits but no more than 1 verified external workflow;
- no independent reproduction attempt exists after two weeks;
- most informed critics identify missing historical-data provenance as the same blocking weakness;
- reproduction exposes nondeterminism, look-ahead, execution, or accounting errors;
- the data license cannot support the promised reproducibility.
