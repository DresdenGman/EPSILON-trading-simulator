# Bring a backtest question

Start in [Discussion #8](https://github.com/DresdenGman/EPSILON-trading-simulator/discussions/8). No code contribution or endorsement is required.

Copy these three prompts:

1. **The claim:** What does the backtest or chart seem to say?
2. **The uncertainty:** Which assumption or conclusion do you distrust, and why?
3. **An optional public example:** Link a chart, public dataset, or reproducible configuration you have permission to share.

Examples of useful questions:

- Does this conclusion survive more realistic fees?
- Is the signal using information that would not yet have been available?
- Did changing the date range change the conclusion, and what can we infer from that?
- Is this a sensitivity check or a genuinely independent test?

## What happens next

A maintainer scopes the question and identifies what can be tested with the current instrument. A useful response may be an experiment, a documented limitation, a request for clarification, or an explanation of why the instrument cannot answer it. We do not promise to solve every submitted question.

For a testable case, retain the original claim, inputs, rejection rule, result or blocker, and public follow-up. Invite the questioner to say whether the response addressed their question; do not infer satisfaction from silence. Code defects and reproduction attempts can then use the relevant [issue form](https://github.com/DresdenGman/EPSILON-trading-simulator/issues/new/choose).

The first-round goal is **three scoped public questions**. It is a target, not a completed-case count.

## Privacy and scope

Use only public or shareable material. Never post API keys, passwords, brokerage details, personal account statements, or restricted market data. Do not seek personalized trade recommendations. A failed result and criticism are welcome; stars and favorable reviews are never participation conditions.
